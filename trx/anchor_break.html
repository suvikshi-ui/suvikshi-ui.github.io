Anchor Break

Buy and sell pattern built on zigzag anchors. Day runs 05:30 to 05:30 IST. Signals only, no trades. Not financial advice.

Rules

BUY

  1. Anchor 1 is the previous day's last zigzag Lower Low (LL).
  2. After Anchor 1, only Higher Highs (HH) may form for the rest of that day.
  3. Anchor 2 is the next day's first low pivot, and it must be an LL below Anchor 1.
  4. Entry is the first 15m candle that closes above Anchor 1.
  5. Stop is the previous candle's low.

SELL

  1. Anchor 1 is the previous day's last zigzag Higher High (HH).
  2. After Anchor 1, only Lower Lows (LL) may form for the rest of that day.
  3. Anchor 2 is the next day's first high pivot, and it must be an HH above Anchor 1.
  4. Entry is the first 15m candle that closes below Anchor 1.
  5. Stop is the previous candle's high.
