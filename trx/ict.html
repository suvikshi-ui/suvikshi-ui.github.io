The rule

Buy

Take ICT buy setups only when the Nifty 50 closes above both its 50-day and 200-day moving averages.

Sell

Take ICT sell setups only when the Nifty 50 closes below both its 50-day and 200-day moving averages.
Sideways filter (active): ADX(14) < 24 → skip the setup. Threshold in use is 24. The NSE ADX 24 backtest is shown below; other NSE tables on this tab are without ADX.

Setup: the low (high) sweeps a prior swing low (high) and closes back inside → within 10 bars, a displacement candle (body ≥ 1.3× its 20-day average) closes beyond the latest swing high (low) → limit entry within 10 bars on a retrace into the fair value gap, or the order block if there is no gap. Exits: ICT stop 2R = stop just beyond the sweep extreme or order block, target 2× risk. 20-day expiry.

What matters (and what does not)

  • The Nifty 200-day average is the main factor. Buys work above it (ICT 2R: 708 trades, PF 1.35); sells only work below it. Sells with the Nifty above it lose heavily (ICT 2R −480%).
  • 2022 lost money (a choppy market around the averages).
  • Stock trend, volatility (ATR%, India VIX), 52-week position, day of week and FVG vs order block entry showed no reliable edge out of sample.

The rule

Buy

ICT buy setups only when BTC closes above its 200 SMA on the same timeframe as the trade.

Sell

ICT sell setups only when BTC closes below its 200 SMA on the same timeframe.
Sideways filter (active): ADX(14) < 24 → skip the setup. Threshold in use is 24. Backtests below were run at ADX 25 and are labelled as such.
  • Universe: 33 BingX USDT perpetual pairs (TradingView data). Timeframe: daily or 4H.
  • Setup: swing points 2 bars each side; sweep of swing low that closes back above; MSS within 10 bars (close above swing high, body ≥ 1.3× 20-bar average); limit entry on retrace into FVG (or order block if no FVG), must fill within 10 bars. Sells mirrored.
  • Exit (version B): stop beyond sweep/order block, target 2R. Expiry 20 days on daily, 30 bars on 4H. 0.1% cost per trade.
