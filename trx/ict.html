The rule

Buy

Take ICT buy setups only when the Nifty 50 closes above both its 50-day and 200-day moving averages.

Sell

Take ICT sell setups only when the Nifty 50 closes below both its 50-day and 200-day moving averages.
Sideways filter (active): ADX(14) < 24 → skip the setup. Threshold in use is 24. All results below use this rule.

Setup: the low (high) sweeps a prior swing low (high) and closes back inside → within 10 bars, a displacement candle (body ≥ 1.3× its 20-day average) closes beyond the latest swing high (low) → limit entry within 10 bars on a retrace into the fair value gap, or the order block if there is no gap. Exits: ICT stop 2R = stop just beyond the sweep extreme or order block, target 2× risk. 20-day expiry.

The rule

Buy

ICT buy setups only when BTC closes above its 200 SMA on the same timeframe as the trade.

Sell

ICT sell setups only when BTC closes below its 200 SMA on the same timeframe.
Sideways filter (active): ADX(14) < 24 → skip the setup. Threshold in use is 24. All results below use this rule.
  • Universe: 33 BingX USDT perpetual pairs (TradingView data). Timeframe: 4H.

  • Exit (version B): stop beyond sweep/order block, target 2R. Expiry 30 bars on 4H. 0.1% cost per trade.
