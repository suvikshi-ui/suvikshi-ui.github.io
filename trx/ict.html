The rule

Buy

Take ICT buy setups only when the Nifty 50 closes above both its 50-day and 200-day moving averages.

Sell

Take ICT sell setups only when the Nifty 50 closes below both its 50-day and 200-day moving averages.

Setup: the low (high) sweeps a prior swing low (high) and closes back inside → within 10 bars, a displacement candle (body ≥ 1.3× its 20-day average) closes beyond the latest swing high (low) → limit entry within 10 bars on a retrace into the fair value gap, or the order block if there is no gap. Exits: 3/6 = fixed 3% stop / 6% target. ICT stop 2R = stop just beyond the sweep extreme or order block, target 2× risk. 20-day expiry.

What matters (and what does not)

  • The Nifty 200-day average is the main factor. Buys work above it (ICT 2R: 708 trades, PF 1.35); sells only work below it. Sells with the Nifty above it lose heavily (3/6 −330%, ICT 2R −480%).
  • 2022 lost money in both versions (a choppy market around the averages).
  • Stock trend, volatility (ATR%, India VIX), 52-week position, day of week and FVG vs order block entry showed no reliable edge out of sample.

Method notes

  • Data: TradingView daily candles (NSE stocks, NSE:NIFTY, NSE:INDIAVIX). Universe: TradingView Nifty 100 list, price ≥ ₹500 at entry. Entries from 1 Jan 2021 to 21 Oct 2026.
  • 0.1% cost per trade. Stats are on closed trades, grouped by entry year. Totals are summed per-trade %, not portfolio returns. Max DD is measured on that running sum.
  • Conditions are measured at the close before entry (no look-ahead). Session timing (London/NY) does not apply to daily NSE candles.
  • Survivorship caveat: the universe is today's Nifty 100 list applied to past years.
  • Fit 2021–23 / test 2024–26: the rule was chosen in the fit period and checked on the later years.
  • Research results, not investment advice. Past results do not guarantee future returns.
