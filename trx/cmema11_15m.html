How to read this. TradingView does not provide 15-minute Renko, so bricks are built from TradingView 15-minute candles with a fixed ATR 14 box size. Cost 0.1% per trade. Totals are the simple sum of per-trade % returns, not the return of one account. Many filter combinations were tested on this one dataset, so the best number is flattered; verification on a separate 3-month window is pending.

Rules

Entry

  1. Renko: close-based, 2-box reversal, box = ATR 14 (Wilder) of the 15m candles just before the window start (fixed for the whole test).
  2. Buy: a green brick right after a red brick that crosses above EMA 11 of brick closes. Sell: a red brick after a green brick crossing below EMA 11.
  3. Entry at the signal brick's close. One trade at a time per stock.
  4. Filter: exactly 2 opposite bricks before the flip brick.

Exit

  1. Stop: body of the last opposite brick before entry (buy: lower of its open/close; sell: higher). Checked on every 15m candle; a gap through the stop fills at the candle open.
  2. Fixed target 1.5R (R = entry to stop). If stop and target are hit in the same candle, the stop is assumed.
  3. EMA exit: a red brick crossing below EMA 11 (buy) or a green brick crossing above it (sell) closes the trade at that brick's close.
  4. P/L % = (exit ÷ entry − 1) × 100 × side − 0.1.

Notes

  • Skip the trend and EMA-slope filters: they barely change the win rate (about 35% either way).
  • Don't skip the first 30 minutes of the session: PF drops to about 1.2, because most of the edge comes at the open.
  • Many combinations were tested on one dataset, so the best number is flattered.
  • Verification on a separate 3-month window is pending.
