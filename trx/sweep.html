22,938 combinations of the Renko + CM_EMA 11 strategy family, tested only with fills you could actually get (candle close, next open, limit, stop-entry, pullback) plus slippage, 0.1% cost per trade, closed trades only, 1 Jan 2021 → 26 Oct 2026 where data allows. Backtest only, TradingView data. Not live signals, not advice.

Verdict: no variant holds PF 2.5 robustly. Several reach PF ≥ 2.5 over 2021–26, but when selected on 2021–24 they drop to PF ~1.5–2.5 on 2025–26 and ~1.2–1.7 before 2021. Most robust: NSE150 daily, buys only, limit at brick, daily close above EMA50 & EMA200, EMA11-cross exit — PF 2.64 in period, 1.93 on 2025–26, 1.68 pre-2021. Win rate ~30%: profit comes from few big winners; expect long losing streaks. Sells failed in every version.
