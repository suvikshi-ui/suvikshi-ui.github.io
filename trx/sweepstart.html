Start Here: Sweep Live strategy guide

Simple Hinglish mein poori strategy, step by step. Live signals Sweep Live page par milte hain.

1. Yeh strategy kya hai?

Yeh 150 NSE stocks par chalti hai: Nifty 100 aur Midcap 50. Data sirf TradingView se aata hai. Har stock ka daily Renko chart banta hai, jiska box size ATR 14 se hota hai. Sirf buy trades hain, sell nahi. Aur sirf un stocks mein, jinka daily close EMA 50 aur EMA 200 dono ke upar ho.

2. Roz ka routine

  1. Market 3:30 baje band hota hai.
  2. Lagbhag 4 baje scanner chalta hai aur Sweep Live page update hota hai.
  3. 4 baje ke baad Sweep Live page kholiye.
  4. New buy signals table dekhiye: stock, entry aur stop.
  5. Agar signal hai, toh agle din se brick level par buy limit lagaiye. Yeh aap khud lagati hain, page koi order nahi lagata.
  6. Open trades table mein apne khule trades check kariye.
  7. Exit signals table mein agar aapka stock aaye, toh us din ke close par exit kariye.

3. Signal ke niyam

  • Lagataar exactly do red bricks ke baad ek nayi green brick bane.
  • Woh green brick, brick wale EMA 11 ke upar cross kare.
  • RSI 14 pachpan se sattar ke beech ho.
  • Daily close EMA 50 aur EMA 200 dono ke upar ho.

4. Entry

Signal ke agle din se brick level par buy limit lagti hai. Yeh teen trading din tak valid rehti hai. Price brick level ko chhoota hai toh limit par fill. Agar din ka open hi brick level se neeche khule, toh open par fill.

Agar teen din mein price brick level tak nahi aaya, ya entry se pehle stop hit ho gaya, toh signal cancel. Aise signals Sweep Live page ke Missed entries table mein dikhte hain.

5. Stop loss

Stop brick level se ek box neeche hota hai, yaani green brick ka open.

6. Exit

Jab pehli red brick EMA 11 ke neeche cross kare, us din ke close par exit. Is strategy mein koi trailing stop nahi hai. Ek stock mein ek waqt par sirf ek position.

7. Position sizing (generic suggestion, backtest nahi kiya)

Dhyan dijiye: is strategy ke liye koi saved sizing rule nahi hai. Yeh ek aam tarika hai, iska backtest nahi hua hai.

Har trade mein apni capital ka ek chhota fixed hissa risk kariye, jaise 0.5 se 1 percent. Risk amount = capital × risk percent. Quantity = risk amount ÷ (entry − stop).

Example: capital 5 lakh, risk 1 percent = 5,000 rupaye. Entry 1,000, stop 980, toh farak 20. Quantity = 5,000 ÷ 20 = 250 shares. Dhyan rahe, 250 × 1,000 = 2.5 lakh lagenge, isliye kul capital se zyada mat lagaiye.

8. Kya ummeed rakhein

  • Backtest 2021 se 2026: 224 trades, lagbhag 29 percent jeet.
  • Profit factor poore samay mein 2.64, aur 2025-26 mein 1.93.
  • Max drawdown lagbhag 57 percent, yeh har trade ke percent ka jod hai, capital ka nahi.
  • 2021 se pehle ka profit factor lagbhag 1.68.
  • Zyada trades haarte hain. Lambi haar ki series aa sakti hai, isliye chhota risk rakhiye aur strategy par bharosa rakhiye.

9. Zaroori baatein

Yeh sirf signals hain, koi trade apne aap nahi hota. Yeh investment advice nahi hai. Purana backtest aage ki kamaai ki guarantee nahi hai.
