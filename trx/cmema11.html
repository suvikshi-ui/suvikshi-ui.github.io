Last updated: 26 Oct 2026, 15:52 IST · live scan of TradingView daily Renko · scanned during NSE market hours (9:15 AM to 3:45 PM IST) and once after the close. A provisional tag marks a signal or EMA exit from a brick that formed on a daily bar that is still open; it can change or disappear before that bar closes.

Rules (real backtest rules). Daily Renko from TradingView (ATR 14, source Close, wicks), each brick a bar; daily OHLC for fills and stops. CM_EMA 11 = EMA 11 of the Renko brick closes. BUY: a green brick after a red brick that closes above the EMA (the previous close was at or below the previous EMA, or the brick body spans the EMA). SELL: the mirror. Entry at the brick close. Stop: the body low (high) of the last opposite brick, checked on daily candles; gaps fill at the open. Exit: an opposite-colour brick crossing the EMA, at its close; stop-and-reverse if that brick is also an opposite signal. No fixed 3% / 6% stop or target, and no trend or ADX filter. 0.1% cost per trade. Totals are the sum of per-trade %, not portfolio returns; ₹ figures assume ₹1,00,000 per trade. Brick-close fills may be optimistic.
