Caution. Entries are taken at the Renko brick close level (same method as the crypto page). On NSE, opening gaps often form several bricks inside one 1h candle, so the real 1h close is usually beyond the brick close (median 0.42% worse). Re-priced at the real candle close, the same trades come out at about 50.9% win, PF 0.92, −26% total. Treat the numbers above as an upper bound.

Rules

Entry

  1. Renko from 1h closes, 2-box reversal, box = ATR 14 (Wilder) of the last 1h candle before each month — reset at every month start.
  2. Buy: green brick right after red bricks, crossing above EMA 11 of brick closes; Sell: mirror.
  3. Exactly 2 opposite bricks before the flip brick.
  4. RSI 14 on the signal 1h candle: buy 55–70, sell 30–45.

Exit

  1. Tight stop at the flip brick's open (1 box risk). Checked on every 1h candle; gap through stop fills at open.
  2. Target 1.5R; stop assumed first if both hit in one candle.
  3. EMA exit on an opposite brick crossing EMA 11.
  4. P/L % = (exit ÷ entry − 1) × 100 × side − 0.1% cost. Totals are sums of trade %.
