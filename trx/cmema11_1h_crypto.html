How to read this. Bricks are built from TradingView 1-hour candles with a fixed ATR 14 box size taken from the candles just before 27 Jul. Cost 0.1% per trade, no slippage. Totals are the simple sum of per-trade % returns, not the return of one account. Same rules as the 15m Nifty page; this window was not used to choose them, but it is still one dataset.

Rules

Entry

  1. Renko: close-based, 2-box reversal, box = ATR 14 (Wilder) of the 1h candles just before the window start (fixed for the whole test).
  2. Buy: a green brick right after a red brick that crosses above EMA 11 of brick closes. Sell: a red brick after a green brick crossing below EMA 11.
  3. Entry at the signal brick's close. One trade at a time per coin.
  4. Filter: exactly 2 opposite bricks before the flip brick.
  5. RSI filter: RSI 14 (Wilder) on the 1h close of the signal candle must be 50–70 for a BUY, or 30–50 for a SELL; otherwise the signal is skipped and the coin stays free for later signals.

Exit

  1. Stop: body of the last opposite brick before entry. Checked on every 1h candle; a gap through the stop fills at the candle open.
  2. Fixed target 1.5R (R = entry to stop). If stop and target are hit in the same candle, the stop is assumed.
  3. EMA exit: an opposite brick crossing EMA 11 closes the trade at that brick's close.
  4. P/L % = (exit ÷ entry − 1) × 100 × side − 0.1.
